Floaty is installed

Watch YouTube in a floating window while you work, with speed and loop controls built in.

Try it on YouTube
  1. 1

    Open any YouTube video

    Click the new Floaty button in the player's control bar, or press Alt+P.

    Alt+P
  2. 2

    Pin Floaty to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle piece at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Floaty.

    Floaty
  3. 3

    Control everything from the window

    Change speed, set an A-B loop or skip 10 seconds without going back to the tab.

    1.5× A-B
